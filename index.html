Our Story

Two brothers, one passion

Since 2007, DaVinci's Ristorante Italiano has proudly served the Anderson community. Founded by the Albouchi family, with a Mediterranean background, the restaurant is led by Moe, who holds a degree in management, and chef Atef, trained in culinary arts.

Together with a dedicated team, they bring generous Italian cooking and a warm dining experience to life every day — fresh pizza, homemade pasta, and classic Italian dishes made from scratch.
